About the Journal

We believe good design is a quiet, daily act of care.

Frames Shape Living is an independent editorial platform exploring the spaces we inhabit — from the structural bones of a building to the texture of a door handle. We publish in-depth articles, critical perspectives, and practical guides for people who think deeply about the built environment.

Founded 2018 Independent · Reader-supported

Why we exist

Too much design media is either superficial or inaccessible. We wanted a publication that treats interiors and architecture with the same seriousness as art, literature, or film — without losing the warmth and practicality that makes a house a home. Every article is written for readers who want to understand not just what looks good, but why it works.

How we work

We are independent and reader-supported. We don’t accept sponsored content disguised as editorial. When we recommend a material, a designer, or a method, it’s because we’ve researched it, tested it, or spoken to people who have lived with it. Our archive spans hundreds of articles, from 800-word explainers to 4,000-word guides.

What guides our editorial

01

Clarity over hype

We avoid jargon and trend-chasing. If a concept is complex, we break it down. If a product is overrated, we say so.

02

Longevity over novelty

We champion design that ages well — materials that patina, layouts that adapt, and ideas that outlast a season.

03

Honesty over access

We publish critical perspectives, not just glossy tours. Our loyalty is to the reader, not to brands or PR firms.

Common questions

Is Frames Shape Living affiliated with any brands or manufacturers? +

No. We are completely independent. We do not accept payment for coverage, and we do not publish sponsored articles. Our funding comes from reader donations and our occasional print anthology.

Can I contribute an article or pitch a story? +

Yes — we welcome pitches from architects, designers, historians, and writers. Please see our contact page for submission guidelines. We pay for accepted work.

Do you have a print edition? +

We publish a limited-run print anthology once a year, collecting our best long-form essays and photography. Subscribe to our newsletter to be notified when the next edition is available.
