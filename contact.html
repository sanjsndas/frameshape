Before you write

Do you accept guest articles or pitches? +

We do, but we’re highly selective. Please send a concise pitch (300 words max) outlining the angle, intended audience, and why it fits our editorial voice. We do not accept pre-written articles.

How quickly do you respond? +

We aim to respond to all legitimate inquiries within 2–3 business days. If you haven’t heard back within a week, feel free to follow up — occasionally messages slip through.

Do you offer design consultations? +

No, Frames Shape Living is an editorial publication. We do not provide design services or referrals. We recommend reaching out to a licensed local architect or interior designer.

I’d like to advertise. Where do I start? +

Please email partners@framesshapeliving.com with a brief introduction and your goals. We’ll send our media kit and current opportunities.
