Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy explains how Frames Shape Living uses cookies and similar technologies to recognise you when you visit our website. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

Last updated: 12 February 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Frames Shape Living’s Cookies Policy. This page explains how we use cookies and similar tracking technologies on framesshapeliving.com (the “Site”). We recommend reading this alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal data more broadly.

By continuing to browse the Site, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy. If you do not agree, you can disable cookies in your browser settings (see section 6).

The short version: We use a small number of essential cookies to make the Site work, and optional analytics cookies to understand how readers use our content. You can opt out of analytics at any time.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Frames Shape Living) are called “first-party cookies.” Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called “third-party cookies.” Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionality to be provided on or through the website (e.g., analytics).

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies on our Site:

Category Purpose Examples
Essential Required for the Site to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas. The Site cannot function without these. Session cookies, cookie consent preference
Analytics Help us understand how visitors interact with the Site by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve our content and user experience. Privacy-friendly analytics (e.g., Plausible, Fathom)
Functional Allow the Site to remember choices you make (such as your language or region) and provide enhanced, more personal features. Language preference, reading list
Marketing Used to track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements. We currently do not use marketing cookies on Frames Shape Living. —

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our Site and to use some of its features. Because these cookies are strictly necessary to deliver the Site, you cannot refuse them without impacting how our Site functions.

3.2 Analytics Cookies

We use privacy-friendly analytics to collect information about how visitors use our Site. We use this information to improve the Site and to understand which articles and topics are most valued by our readers. The data collected is anonymised and aggregated — we do not use it to identify individual visitors.

3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the Site to remember choices you make when you use the Site, such as remembering your reading preferences. The purpose of these cookies is to provide you with a more personal experience and to avoid you having to re-enter your preferences every time you visit.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by adjusting your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies you have and delete them on an individual basis;
  • Block third-party cookies;
  • Block cookies from particular sites;
  • Block all cookies from being set;
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

If you choose to block or delete cookies, some features of the Site may not work as intended. For example, you may not be able to access certain parts of the Site, and your preferences may not be remembered.

To find out more about how to manage cookies, visit aboutcookies.org or allaboutcookies.org.

5. Third-Party Cookies

In some special cases, we also use cookies provided by trusted third parties. The following section details which third-party cookies you might encounter through this Site:

  • Analytics providers: We use privacy-focused analytics that do not set cookies or track individual users. No personal data is shared with these providers.
  • Newsletter services: If you subscribe to our newsletter, our email service provider may set cookies to help us manage subscriptions and track open rates.
  • Embedded content: Articles on our Site may include embedded content (e.g., videos, images, articles). Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if you visited the other website directly.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.
