Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between interior design and interior architecture? +

Interior design focuses on finishes, furniture, color, and styling within existing spaces. Interior architecture involves altering the structural or spatial layout — moving walls, adding windows, redesigning circulation. In practice, the best projects blur the line.

How do I choose the right lighting for a living room? +

Layer your lighting: ambient (general), task (reading), and accent (highlighting art or architecture). Use warm bulbs (2700–3000K) and dimmers. Avoid a single central ceiling light — it flattens the room.

What are the most sustainable materials for a home renovation? +

Reclaimed timber, cork, bamboo, recycled steel, clay plaster, and low-VOC paints are excellent choices. Also consider the embodied carbon and local sourcing. Durability is key — a material that lasts 50 years is more sustainable than a biodegradable one that needs replacing every 5 years.

How can I make a small room feel larger? +

Use light colors, large mirrors, and consistent flooring. Avoid heavy curtains and bulky furniture. Opt for multi-functional pieces and vertical storage. Lighting should be layered and bright but not harsh.
